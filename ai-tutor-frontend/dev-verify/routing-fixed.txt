## 已知真 bug (mock-only 设计除外)

frontend `rag.js` / `exam.js` / `user.js` / `tutor.js` 路径错乱已修 (本次 commit):
- `POST /api/rag/search/search` → `POST /api/rag/search` (去多余 /search)
- `POST /api/rag/search/ingest` → `POST /api/rag/ingest`
- `POST /api/rag/search/multi/*` → `POST /api/rag/multi/*`
- `GET /api/rag/search/stats` → `GET /api/rag/stats`
- `POST /api/rag/multi/questions` → `POST /api/rag/multi/questions/:questionId` (加 :questionId)
- `GET /api/exam-pdf/` → `POST /api/exam/pdf/generate/:paperId` (改 method + 路径)
- `GET/POST /api/user/user-province` → `GET/POST /api/auth/prefs/province`
- `rag.explain` / `rag.ask` 加 mock-only 守卫 (后端无对应端点)
- `tutor.getHistory()` 加本地存储注释 (mock 模式)
- 后端 `exam/routes.js`: 加 `:paperId` 占位符
