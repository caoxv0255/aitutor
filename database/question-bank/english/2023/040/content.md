## 参考答案

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to apply for the position of volunteer English teacher advertised in the newspaper. I am a college student majoring in English, and I have a good command of English. I have also participated in similar activities before, which makes me qualified for this job. I am confident that I can help students improve their English. I would be grateful if you could give me a chance.

Yours sincerely,
Li Hua

## 解析

本题考查应用文写作。要求写一封申请信。需包含：自我介绍、申请原因、自身优势。注意格式：称呼、正文、结束语、签名。评分标准：内容完整（4分），语言准确（3分），格式正确（2分），连贯得体（1分）。
