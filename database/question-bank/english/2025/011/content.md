# 第11题

**题型**: 填空题

**难度**: ★★☆☆☆

**分值**: 4.5分

**知识点**: 形容词、非谓语动词、表语从句

---

## 题目内容

Most days after school, I love walking in the forest with my dog Nick. It’s ____11____ (peace), especially around sunset. Nick has a fun time smelling the leaves and running after rabbits. When Nick runs ahead, I hear him barking excitedly. I sometimes call him back, worried that he might have someone ____12____ (scare). The truth, though, is ____13____ could be guessed — there’s never anyone else here. Just me, Nick, and the quiet forest.

## 小题

### 11

It’s ____11____ (peace), especially around sunset.

**答案**: peaceful

### 12

I sometimes call him back, worried that he might have someone ____12____ (scare).

**答案**: scared

### 13

The truth, though, is ____13____ could be guessed — there’s never anyone else here.

**答案**: what

---

## 参考答案

11. peaceful; 12. scared; 13. what

## 解析

11. 考查形容词。句意：这里很宁静，尤其是在日落时分。“is”后接形容词作表语，“peace”的形容词形式是“peaceful”，意为“宁静的”。故填peaceful。12. 考查非谓语动词。句意：我有时会把他叫回来，担心他可能会吓到别人。“have sb. done”表示“使某人被……”， “someone”与“scare”之间是被动关系，即某人被吓到，用过去分词作宾语补足语。故填scared。13. 考查表语从句引导词。句意：不过，真相正如所料——这里从来没有其他人。“is”后接表语从句，从句中缺少主语，指物，用what引导，what在从句中作主语，意为“……的事情”。故填what。
