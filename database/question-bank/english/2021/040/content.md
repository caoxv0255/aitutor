## 参考答案

Dear Tom,

I'm writing to invite you to the English Corner this Friday evening. It will be held in the school library from 7:00 to 9:00 p.m. We'll discuss the topic "Environmental Protection" and share our ideas. I think it's a great chance to practice English and make new friends. I hope you can come.

Looking forward to your reply.

Yours,
Li Hua

## 解析

本题考查书面表达。写作要点：1. 邀请参加英语角；2. 时间地点；3. 活动内容；4. 表达期待。评分标准：内容完整（4分），语言准确（3分），结构清晰（2分），书写规范（1分）。范文覆盖所有要点，使用正确时态和句式，如现在进行时表将来、定语从句等。
